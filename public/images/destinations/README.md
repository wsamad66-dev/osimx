## 🎨 Image Guidelines

### Composition
- **Perspective**: Bird's eye view or airplane window perspective
- **Mood**: Inviting, aspirational, clear weather preferred
- **Colors**: Vibrant but natural, avoid heavy filters

### Technical Specs
- **Format**: JPG or WebP
- **Resolution**: Minimum 1200x900px (4:3 aspect ratio)
- **File size**: Optimize to under 500KB each
- **Orientation**: Landscape (horizontal)

### Optimization
Use tools like:
- **TinyPNG**: https://tinypng.com/
- **Squoosh**: https://squoosh.app/
- **ImageOptim** (Mac): https://imageoptim.com/
